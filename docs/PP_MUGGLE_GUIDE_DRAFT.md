# Plonk & Play — MUGGLE Guide
**Two-page in-box Quick Start draft 0.1 · 20 November 2026 · Pre-release**

## Page 1 — PLONK. PLUG. PLAY.

### 1. PLONK — position your sensors
Place one sensor on each lane, in the recommended position before the Start/Finish line. Match the direction and positions in the supplied installation illustration.

[Large labelled illustration: standard two-lane installation, Sensor 1 and Sensor 2, direction of travel, Start/Finish line and starting positions. Replace with hardware-verified illustration.]

### 2. PLUG — connect P&P
With power disconnected, connect Sensor 1 to Lane 1 and Sensor 2 to Lane 2 using the labelled connections. Connect the specified power supply. Keep cables clear of the cars.

[Photograph: actual labelled connections and supplied power connection.]

Switch on and wait for **READY**.

### 3. PLAY — race!
- Choose **LAPS** or **ENDURANCE** on the touchscreen.
- Set the distance or duration. The factory choices are 10 laps and 5 minutes.
- Position the cars and press **START**.
- Wait for the five red lights to turn green, then go!
- When the race is finished, press **RACE AGAIN** to prepare another race. Press **START** when ready.

[Production READY screenshot with three callouts: mode, value, START.]

**Need to stop?** Hold PAUSE for at least 0.7 seconds and release. Choose RESUME or ABANDON RACE.

No phone, Internet, racer registration or track profile is needed for this basic two-lane installation.

## Page 2 — Want more? Connect to Race Control.

### 4. CONNECT — use your phone, tablet or computer

1. Open Wi-Fi settings and select the P&P network name shown on your unit.
2. The factory network is open: no Wi-Fi password is needed. If your device says there is no Internet, stay connected.
3. Open your normal web browser and enter the local address displayed by P&P.
4. Race Control opens. Use the controlling Browser to choose a race and change settings.

[Production photo and Browser screenshot: where to find network name/address, and Home screen.]

**You're ready to explore.** Choose Lap Race, Open Practice or Endurance. Add racer names, choose your layout and explore Results & History. Eligible Browser-controlled physical races can be recorded; standalone touchscreen races and Practice do not create official records.

P&P can also join your house or club Wi-Fi through Race Control's Network settings. You do not need it to race.

### Not quite working?

| What you see | Try this |
|---|---|
| Not READY | Check power and the two sensor connections. |
| A car is not counted | Check sensor position, obstruction and the matching lane connection. |
| Browser not opening | Check you joined P&P Wi-Fi and entered the exact displayed address. |
| Controls unavailable | Check whether another Browser is controlling P&P. |
